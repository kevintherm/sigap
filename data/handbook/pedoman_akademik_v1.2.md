## 3.1 | Kehadiran Minimum | Minimum Attendance
tags: hadir, kehadiran, absen, absensi, presensi, attendance, minimal, minimum, persen, 75, syarat, uas, ujian, daring, online
ID: Mahasiswa wajib hadir minimal 75% dari pertemuan setiap mata kuliah. Untuk kelas daring, kehadiran dihitung dari aktivitas yang tercatat di LMS (mengerjakan aktivitas mingguan atau mengikuti sesi sinkron). Mahasiswa dengan kehadiran di bawah 75% tidak boleh mengikuti UAS mata kuliah tersebut.
EN: Students must attend at least 75% of the meetings of each course. For online classes, attendance is counted from activity recorded in the LMS (completing the weekly activity or joining the live session). Students below 75% attendance may not sit the final exam (UAS) for that course.

## 3.2 | Izin Tidak Hadir | Excused Absence
tags: izin, sakit, surat, dokter, absen, hadir, permission, sick, excused, absence, keterangan, darurat, emergency
ID: Ketidakhadiran karena sakit atau keadaan darurat harus dilaporkan lewat menu Izin di portal akademik paling lambat 3 hari kerja setelah tidak hadir, disertai surat keterangan dokter atau bukti pendukung. Izin yang disetujui tidak mengurangi persentase kehadiran, dengan batas paling banyak 3 pertemuan per mata kuliah.
EN: Absence due to illness or an emergency must be reported through the Permission menu in the academic portal within 3 working days, with a doctor's note or other supporting evidence. Approved permissions do not reduce the attendance percentage, up to a maximum of 3 meetings per course.

## 4.1 | Pengumpulan Tugas Lewat LMS | Submitting Assignments Through the LMS
tags: kumpul, unggah, upload, submit, tugas, lms, email, kanal, resmi, gangguan, error, down, format, file
ID: Semua tugas dikumpulkan lewat LMS sesuai tenggat yang tertulis di halaman mata kuliah. Tugas yang dikirim lewat email atau WhatsApp tidak dihitung, kecuali LMS mengalami gangguan yang diumumkan resmi oleh Direktorat TI; dalam kondisi itu tugas dikirim ke email dosen sebelum tenggat dengan menyertakan tangkapan layar gangguan.
EN: All assignments are submitted through the LMS by the deadline shown on the course page. Work sent by email or WhatsApp is not counted, unless the LMS has an outage officially announced by the IT Directorate; in that case the work goes to the lecturer's email before the deadline together with a screenshot of the error.

## 4.2 | Keterlambatan Pengumpulan Tugas | Late Assignment Submission
tags: terlambat, telat, late, kumpul, submit, tugas, assignment, potong, potongan, penalti, penalty, nilai, dinilai, graded, lewat, tenggat
ID: Tugas yang terlambat tetap dinilai dengan potongan: terlambat sampai 24 jam dipotong 20%, terlambat lebih dari 24 jam sampai 72 jam dipotong 50%, dan lebih dari 72 jam mendapat nilai 0. Potongan tidak berlaku bila mahasiswa sudah mendapat perpanjangan tenggat sesuai Pasal 4.3. Kuis dan ujian tidak dapat dikerjakan terlambat.
EN: Late assignments are still graded with a penalty: up to 24 hours late loses 20%, more than 24 and up to 72 hours late loses 50%, and more than 72 hours late scores 0. No penalty applies if the student was granted an extension under Article 4.3. Quizzes and exams cannot be taken late.

## 4.3 | Perpanjangan Tenggat Tugas | Assignment Extensions
tags: perpanjang, perpanjangan, extension, extend, tenggat, deadline, tambahan, waktu, minta, request, dosen, alasan, sakit, darurat
ID: Perpanjangan tenggat diajukan langsung kepada dosen pengampu sebelum tenggat berakhir, dengan alasan dan bukti (misalnya sakit, musibah, atau tugas kantor bagi mahasiswa kelas karyawan). Perpanjangan paling lama 7 hari dan keputusan sepenuhnya ada pada dosen. Permintaan setelah tenggat lewat tidak diproses.
EN: Extensions are requested directly from the course lecturer before the deadline passes, with a reason and evidence (for example illness, bereavement, or work duties for working-student classes). An extension is at most 7 days and the decision rests entirely with the lecturer. Requests made after the deadline are not processed.

## 4.4 | Integritas Akademik dan Penggunaan AI | Academic Integrity and AI Use
tags: plagiat, plagiarisme, plagiarism, curang, nyontek, contek, cheating, ai, chatgpt, joki, integritas, integrity, sanksi, sanction, salin, copy
ID: Plagiarisme, menyontek, menggunakan jasa joki, dan menyerahkan karya buatan AI sebagai karya sendiri adalah pelanggaran integritas akademik. Penggunaan alat AI hanya boleh bila diizinkan dosen dan wajib disebutkan. Sanksi bertingkat mulai dari nilai 0 untuk tugas tersebut, nilai E untuk mata kuliah, sampai skorsing satu semester.
EN: Plagiarism, cheating, using a ghost-writing service, and submitting AI-generated work as one's own are violations of academic integrity. AI tools may be used only when the lecturer allows it, and their use must be disclosed. Sanctions escalate from a 0 for the assignment, to an E for the course, up to a one-semester suspension.

## 5.1 | Ujian Susulan | Make-up Exams
tags: susulan, makeup, ujian, uts, uas, sakit, ikut, missed, miss, absen, darurat, kaprodi, ajukan
ID: Mahasiswa yang tidak dapat mengikuti UTS atau UAS karena sakit, musibah, atau tugas negara dapat mengajukan ujian susulan lewat portal akademik paling lambat 2 hari kerja setelah jadwal ujian, dengan bukti pendukung. Pengajuan disetujui oleh Ketua Program Studi dan ujian susulan tidak dipungut biaya.
EN: Students who cannot sit a midterm (UTS) or final (UAS) because of illness, bereavement, or state duty may request a make-up exam through the academic portal no later than 2 working days after the exam, with supporting evidence. Requests are approved by the Head of Study Program and make-up exams are free of charge.

## 5.2 | Syarat Mengikuti Ujian | Exam Eligibility
tags: syarat, ujian, uts, uas, ikut, eligible, eligibility, kartu, ukt, bayar, lunas, hadir, requirement, boleh
ID: Untuk mengikuti UTS dan UAS mahasiswa harus terdaftar di mata kuliah pada KRS, tidak memiliki tunggakan UKT untuk cicilan yang sudah jatuh tempo, dan memiliki kartu ujian digital dari portal akademik. Khusus UAS, kehadiran minimal 75% (Pasal 3.1) juga berlaku.
EN: To sit the midterm (UTS) and final (UAS), students must be registered for the course in their KRS, have no overdue tuition (UKT) installments, and hold the digital exam card from the academic portal. For the final exam, the 75% minimum attendance (Article 3.1) also applies.

## 5.3 | Tata Tertib Ujian Daring | Online Exam Rules
tags: ujian, daring, online, kamera, camera, pengawas, proctoring, proctor, terlambat, telat, late, koneksi, internet, putus, tata, tertib
ID: Selama ujian daring kamera wajib menyala dan proctoring LMS aktif. Mahasiswa yang terlambat lebih dari 15 menit tidak boleh mengikuti ujian. Bila koneksi terputus, mahasiswa segera melapor ke pengawas lewat kanal yang diumumkan dan dapat melanjutkan ujian dalam sisa waktu yang ada.
EN: During online exams the camera must stay on and LMS proctoring must be active. Students more than 15 minutes late may not sit the exam. If the connection drops, the student reports to the proctor through the announced channel straight away and may continue within the remaining time.

## 6.1 | Skala Nilai | Grading Scale
tags: skala, nilai, huruf, angka, grade, grading, scale, ipk, gpa, bobot, lulus, pass, minimum
ID: Nilai huruf ditetapkan dari nilai angka: A (85–100, bobot 4,0), AB (78–84, 3,5), B (70–77, 3,0), BC (63–69, 2,5), C (55–62, 2,0), D (45–54, 1,0), dan E (di bawah 45, 0). Nilai minimal lulus mata kuliah adalah C.
EN: Letter grades come from numeric scores: A (85–100, weight 4.0), AB (78–84, 3.5), B (70–77, 3.0), BC (63–69, 2.5), C (55–62, 2.0), D (45–54, 1.0) and E (below 45, 0). The minimum passing grade for a course is C.

## 6.2 | Banding Nilai Akhir | Final Grade Appeals
tags: banding, appeal, keberatan, nilai, akhir, final, salah, koreksi, review, dosen, kaprodi
ID: Keberatan atas nilai akhir mata kuliah diajukan lewat formulir Banding Nilai di portal akademik dalam masa banding yang tercantum di kalender akademik. Dosen menanggapi paling lambat 5 hari kerja; bila mahasiswa belum puas, banding diteruskan ke Ketua Program Studi yang keputusannya bersifat final.
EN: Objections to a final course grade are filed with the Grade Appeal form in the academic portal during the appeal window listed in the academic calendar. The lecturer responds within 5 working days; if the student is still not satisfied, the appeal goes to the Head of Study Program, whose decision is final.

## 6.3 | Mengulang Mata Kuliah | Retaking a Course
tags: ulang, mengulang, retake, repeat, remedial, perbaikan, improve, d, e, gagal, fail, tidak lulus
ID: Mata kuliah dengan nilai D atau E dapat diulang pada semester berikutnya saat mata kuliah itu ditawarkan, dengan mengambilnya kembali di KRS. Nilai yang dipakai adalah nilai terbaik. Tidak ada ujian remedial di luar jadwal kecuali diadakan oleh dosen untuk seluruh kelas.
EN: Courses graded D or E can be retaken in a later semester when the course is offered, by adding it to the KRS again. The best grade counts. There are no individual remedial exams unless the lecturer holds one for the whole class.

## 7.1 | Cuti Akademik | Academic Leave
tags: cuti, leave, berhenti, sementara, istirahat, formulir, prosedur, cara, ajukan, apply, dekan, biaya
ID: Cuti akademik diajukan lewat formulir Cuti di portal akademik dalam masa pengajuan cuti pada kalender akademik, setelah disetujui dosen wali. Cuti paling lama 2 semester selama masa studi, tidak dihitung dalam masa studi, dan mahasiswa membayar biaya cuti sebesar 25% UKT. Persetujuan akhir diberikan oleh Dekan.
EN: Academic leave is requested through the Leave form in the academic portal during the leave-application window in the academic calendar, after approval from the academic advisor. Leave may total at most 2 semesters, does not count toward the study period, and the student pays a leave fee of 25% of tuition (UKT). Final approval is given by the Dean.

## 7.2 | Pembatalan Mata Kuliah | Course Withdrawal
tags: batal, pembatalan, withdraw, withdrawal, drop, mundur, refund, transkrip, ajukan
ID: Mahasiswa dapat membatalkan mata kuliah setelah masa perubahan KRS, selama masa pembatalan mata kuliah di kalender akademik. Mata kuliah yang dibatalkan tercatat dengan nilai W di transkrip, tidak dihitung dalam IPK, dan biaya tidak dikembalikan.
EN: Students may withdraw from a course after the add/drop period, during the course-withdrawal window in the academic calendar. A withdrawn course appears as W on the transcript, does not count toward the GPA, and fees are not refunded.

## 7.3 | Pengisian dan Perubahan KRS | Course Registration and Add/Drop
tags: krs, registrasi, registration, register, enroll, isi, tambah, add, drop, ubah, perubahan, sks, beban, maksimal, dosen, wali
ID: KRS diisi lewat portal akademik dalam masa pengisian KRS dan disetujui dosen wali. Beban maksimal 24 SKS bila IP semester sebelumnya 3,00 atau lebih, dan 20 SKS bila di bawahnya. Mata kuliah dapat ditambah atau dibatalkan tanpa catatan selama masa perubahan KRS.
EN: The KRS is filled in through the academic portal during the course-registration window and approved by the academic advisor. The maximum load is 24 credits if the previous semester GPA is 3.00 or higher, and 20 credits otherwise. Courses can be added or dropped without a record during the add/drop period.

## 8.1 | Pembayaran UKT dan Cicilan | Tuition Payment and Installments
tags: ukt, bayar, pembayaran, tuition, fee, payment, cicil, cicilan, installment, tunggakan, telat, terlambat, denda, va, virtual
ID: UKT dapat dibayar penuh atau dalam 3 cicilan sesuai jadwal di kalender akademik, lewat virtual account di portal keuangan. Mahasiswa dengan cicilan yang lewat jatuh tempo tidak dapat mengisi KRS dan tidak mendapat kartu ujian sampai tunggakan dilunasi. Keringanan pembayaran diajukan ke Biro Keuangan.
EN: Tuition (UKT) can be paid in full or in 3 installments following the academic calendar, through the virtual account in the finance portal. Students with an overdue installment cannot fill in the KRS and do not receive an exam card until the balance is paid. Payment relief is requested from the Finance Bureau.

## 9.1 | Layanan Mahasiswa | Student Services
tags: layanan, service, services, kontak, contact, jam, hours, kantor, office, helpdesk, akademik, bagian

EN: Academic Services answers academic questions by email at layanan.akademik@und.ac.id and through the online counter in the portal, Monday–Friday 08:00–16:00 WIB. Finance questions go to the Finance Bureau (keuangan@und.ac.id) and account or LMS problems to the IT Helpdesk (helpdesk@und.ac.id).
