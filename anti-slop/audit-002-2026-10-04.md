# antislop audit 002 (2026-10-04): admin panel

Mode: after (session override). Scope: the admin panel only (`sigap/static/admin/`), after the DESIGN.md
restyle. Priority follows the rule tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality Locks = LOW.
Nothing has been changed. Reply with the numbers to fix.

How it was checked: headless Chromium, signed in as admin, all 9 views (inbox with a ticket open, dashboard,
students, schedule, calendar, handbook, users, test chat, account) plus the login page, at 1280 px and 375 px,
in light and dark mode. Measured: page overflow, size of every control, the contrast of every visible text
element against its real background, Tab order from the top of the page, the phone menu, Escape on dialogs,
the test chat while waiting, and console errors. Colour pairs were also run through the antislop contrast
checker. Code was read for error handling, emoji and em dashes.

## HIGH (Hard Gate)

1. **R-32: tickets and students can only be opened with a mouse** (still open from audit 001 #3). Ticket rows
   (`app.js:136`) and student rows (`app.js:244`) are `<tr onclick>` with nothing focusable inside; the probe
   found no keyboard way to open a ticket, which is the inbox's main job. Fix: a real button or link per row
   (for example the reference number), or a focusable row that opens on Enter and Space.

2. **R-32: on phones the closed menu swallows the first 10 Tab presses** (still open from audit 001 #4). At
   375 px, Tab from the top lands on all 9 menu links and "Keluar" while they are off-screen and invisible.
   The ☰ button has no `aria-expanded`, and Escape does not close the open menu. Fix: make the closed menu
   `inert`, add `aria-expanded`, close on Escape, move focus into the menu on open and back to ☰ on close.

3. **R-32: the focus outline is too faint in dark mode** (introduced by the restyle). The outline uses indigo
   `#4F46E5` in both themes; on dark panels (`#141416`) that is 2.93:1, under the 3:1 minimum for a focus
   indicator (light mode is 6.29:1). Fix: in dark mode draw the outline in the light indigo already used for
   dark-mode links, `#A5B4FC` (9.23:1).

4. **R-03: controls are smaller than the 44 px touch minimum, and this collides with DESIGN.md.** Measured at
   375 px: small buttons 32 px (☰, Sinkronkan, filter chips, Ubah, Undangan, Lihat, Reset), regular buttons
   38 px, inputs and selects 42 px; on the schedule page 31 of 44 controls are under 44 px. DESIGN.md asks for
   exactly these sizes (32 / 38 / 44 px), so per R-37 this needs your decision: keep the DESIGN.md sizes, or
   (suggested) apply a 44 px minimum on touch screens only (`@media (pointer: coarse)`), which leaves the
   desktop panel exactly as designed.

5. **R-27: test chat shows nothing while the agent is thinking.** After you send a message, only your own
   bubble appears until the reply arrives, which can take 30 seconds or more; the Send button stays enabled,
   so a second click sends the question twice (`app.js:412-416`). Fix: a "Sigap sedang mengetik…" bubble and
   a disabled Send button until the reply or an error arrives.

6. **R-27: four actions fail silently.** Opening a ticket (`app.js:136`), opening a student (`:244`), viewing
   a handbook version (`:348`) and signing out (`:99`) call the API without error handling: if the request
   fails (expired session excepted), nothing happens and nothing is shown. Fix: route them through the
   existing `guard()` helper, which shows the error as a toast.

7. **R-02: an em dash in the test chat.** The deadline list in test chat joins item and course with " — "
   (`app.js:418`). The bot texts shown there also carry em dashes from the tools (audit 001 #9). Fix: use a
   comma or "·" here, and fix the tool texts with #9.

## MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)

8. **R-04: emoji used as decoration** (still open from audit 001 #11). 18 emoji across the panel: one on every
   menu item (📥 📊 🎓 📅 🗓️ 📖 👥 💬 🔑), ⚠️ on error toasts, 🎓 in the ticket detail, 📅 📖 🔧 in test chat,
   ✓ on delivered replies. They add colour noise next to the indigo-only system in DESIGN.md. Fix: remove them
   from the menu and labels; keep a text "terkirim" for replies.

## LOW (Quality Locks and consistency)

9. **DESIGN.md: three font weights on most screens.** The guide says "no more than two font weights on a single
   screen", but every view uses DM Sans 400 and 500 plus General Sans 700 for headings. Your call: treat the
   heading face as separate (keep), or drop the 500 and use 400 + 700 only.

10. **R-11 / consistency: mixed control heights in one row.** In the ticket header the status select (42 px)
    sits next to "Ambil" (32 px); the schedule filter select (42 px) sits next to a 38 px button. Fix: one
    height per row (selects in a row with small buttons get the small size).

11. **C-4: on phones, sync status is colour only** (still open from audit 001 #12). Below 720 px the
    "Langflow: …" text is hidden and only a green or red dot remains. Fix: a short label or an accessible name.

12. **R-27 (quality): empty states don't say what to do** (still open from audit 001 #15), for example
    "Tidak ada tiket di sini." and "Belum ada item.". Fix: add the cause and the one action that fills it.

13. **C-3 / R-20: the dashboard is the default dashboard shape.** Five equal stat tiles, then charts. The
    actionable number (unanswered questions, which point at handbook gaps) has the same weight as the totals.
    Fix: lead with unanswered questions and open tickets, and make the totals secondary.

## Checked and passing

- Contrast: every visible text element on the login page and all 9 views passes WCAG AA in light and dark
  mode, at both widths (automated, against each element's real background).
- No horizontal overflow at 375 px or 1280 px, in either theme; no console errors.
- Dialogs close with Escape; the native `<dialog>` keeps focus inside.
- Decoration doses match DESIGN.md and the owner's decisions: glow only on primary-button hover (R-13),
  backdrop blur only on the top bar (R-10), the dot grid only behind the login card (R-07, reason recorded).
- Radii follow DESIGN.md (6 px controls, 8 px panels, 12 px login card and dialogs, pill chips); shadows only
  on hover, dialogs and toasts (R-12).
- Indigo appears only on interactive elements; static items use ink, grey or status colours (R-29).
- Dashboard numbers come from the database; no invented statistics (R-17).
